Sothis 0.8.1 shows every election, not only the next national one.

- People & parties → a party now has an **Elections** section grouped into Now, Upcoming and Previous, covering national, provincial and municipal elections. The Assembly overview shows the same timeline.
- The 4 November 2026 Local Government Elections appear as in progress. Status follows South African time: special voting, polling day, voting now (polls 07:00–21:00 SAST), counting, then final once results are declared. No party share is shown until the IEC declares results.
- Previous results: 2024 and 2019 National Assembly ballot shares with seats and turnout, and 2021 municipal ward + PR shares (a secondary compilation of IEC results, labelled as such). Each entry links its sources.
- Upcoming: the 2029 National and Provincial Elections (date not yet proclaimed), carrying the national outlook. No win probability is assigned.
- The engine validates election entries, and source recapture keeps the reviewed calendar.
